What this script does.

Pulls Ontario health news from official sources only.

Sources include Ministry of Health, Ministry of Long Term Care, Regulatory Registry, and related RSS feeds.

Runs a full backfill on first run.

On later cron runs, only processes new items never seen before.

What happens for each new item.

Extracts title, date, ministry, excerpt, and link.

Sends the item to the ChatGPT API.

Generates a clean one sentence summary focused on lab and health system impact.

Explains why the update matters for LifeLabs style decision making.

Extracts dollar amounts if funding or billing is mentioned.

Converts counties or regions into clear Ontario city names.

Assigns an importance score from 0 to 100.

Tags the item into fixed health policy topics.

Scoring logic.

Base score comes from the AI.

Optional keyword rules adjust the score up or down.

Rules live in scoring_rules.json so you edit them without touching code.

Output.

Writes all items, raw plus enriched, into combined_feed.json.

Pushes only new enriched items into the RSS feed endpoint.

Never reposts the same item twice.

Operational behavior.

Safe to run on a cron schedule.

First run fills history.

Future runs append only new news.

No Google Sheets, no URL monitoring, no Discord, no email.

Why this design.

Keeps AI logic centralized in Python.

Keeps the feed clean and decision focused.

Makes scoring and tuning fast to change.

Avoids duplicate posts and wasted API calls.
